I'm a postdoctoral researcher in **computational cognitive neuroscience** working with [Benjamin Morillon](https://ins-amu.fr/benjaminmorillon) at the *Institut de Neurosciences des Systèmes* (Aix-Marseille University) and [Valentin Wyart](https://www.lnc2.fr/team-leader/valentin-wyart/) at the *Laboratoire de Neurosciences Cognitives et Computationnelles* (ENS-PSL, Paris).

I study how the human brain integrates and compresses information over time, from auditory sequences in neonates to perceptual decisions in adults, combining **EEG, MEG, intracranial recordings** and **Bayesian / artificial neural network models**.

I did my PhD at NeuroSpin (CEA & Sorbonne University) under the supervision of [Ghislaine Dehaene-Lambertz](https://neurospin.cea.fr/ghislaine-dehaene-lambertz/), after engineering studies at *CentraleSupélec* and an MSc in computational biology at *Paris-Saclay University*.
